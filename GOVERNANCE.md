# Governance

bbot and the Agent Face Protocol are run in the open by their maintainers,
listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).

## Decisions

- **Library changes**: one maintainer approves and merges.
- **Spec changes**: proposed as an issue labelled `spec`, open for comment for
  at least 14 days, then merged with approval from a majority of maintainers.
  Breaking changes bump the spec's major version.
- Disagreements are settled by discussion first, then by a majority vote of
  maintainers.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors with a record of good, sustained work can be nominated by any
maintainer and are added with a majority vote. Maintainers who are inactive
for six months may step down to emeritus.

The project aims to have maintainers from more than one organisation. Until
it does, that is the top priority for growing this list.

## Changes to this document

Changing governance follows the same process as a spec change.
